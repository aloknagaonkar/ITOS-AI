# Injection-Pinbar (Bottom)

Bullish reversal Candle DNA pattern. Candle colour is ignored.

## Accepted structures

### Classic Injection
- Body >= 55% of candle range
- Upper wick <= 12% of candle range
- Lower injection wick >= 0.30 x real-body size

### Small-Tip Injection
- Body >= 85% of candle range
- Upper wick <= 3% of candle range
- A very small but visible lower tip: > 0% and <= 8% of candle range

## Context
- Candle is at a recent local bottom or follows a short downmove
- Direction is always interpreted as bullish, regardless of candle colour

The pattern contributes a bullish CE vote, but institutional confirmation, false-breakout, stability, flow-validation, and risk gates remain authoritative.
