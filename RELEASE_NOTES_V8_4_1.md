# ITOS v8.4.1 — AI Decision Center

## Added

- New **AI Trade Opportunity** signature section immediately below Key Market Levels.
- Best-ranked contract with AI score, calibrated confidence, decision and institutional context.
- Unified AI Trade Plan with entry zone, stop loss, targets and risk-reward.
- Explainable AI evidence and active blockers.
- Side-by-side Top 5 CE and Top 5 PE candidate tables.
- Modular trade models, orchestration engine and reusable Streamlit UI component.

## Compatibility

- Existing recommendation, confidence, institutional flow, planner and historical engines remain unchanged.
- Existing detailed panels remain available below the new decision center.
- Existing two-tab interface is preserved.
